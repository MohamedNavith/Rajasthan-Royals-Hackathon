### Quantitative Evaluation Metrics:
* **Win Probability Leverage Index ($WPLI$):** Deliveries where ball outcomes create $\Delta WP > 0.052$ (top 25% quantile).
* **Runs Over Expected ($\text{ROE}$):** Contextual run contribution relative to league baseline across phase, bowler type, and venue.
* **Pressure Delta ($\Delta \text{SR}$ / $\Delta \text{Econ}$):**
  $$\Delta \text{SR} = \text{SR}_{\text{pressure}} - \text{SR}_{\text{normal}}$$
  $$\Delta \text{Econ} = \text{Econ}_{\text{normal}} - \text{Econ}_{\text{pressure}}$$
